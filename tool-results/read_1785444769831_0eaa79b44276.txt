   277→   277→**Root Cause**: System-level environment variable `DATABASE_URL=file:/home/z/my-project/db/custom.db` (SQLite) was overriding the `.env` file's `DATABASE_URL=postgresql://z:Cryptsk2026@localhost:5432/ispplatform`.    279→   279→**Impact**: ALL Prisma-dependent API routes were failing:

   281→   281→- `/api/freeradius/sync-status` → 500 PrismaClientInitializationError
